One game, one book, one vocabulary.

This page is the index. The Network tab shows both brains on one screen.

Each brain in this network is its own map: Grand Theft Auto has the series, GTA VI and Leonida, the previewed VI systems, the GTA V and Online economy, Rockstar and the community; Leo's Black Book, the auxiliary brain, has the book site, the seed ledger, Leo the bot, The Lot, the content engine and hosting. What joins them is a set of … shared neurons in the GTA economy and Sources & canon lobes: the same id, title and definition in every brain, each brain adding its own synapses. Open a shared neuron in one brain and the side panel offers the same neuron in the other.
