What the notation and projections mean

These are transliterations of shapes, not translations or known sounds. A dot marks a space, a comma an uncertain space, square brackets alternative readings, braces a ligature and question marks illegible text. Capitals and extended codes must not silently become “known letters” (IVTFF documentation).

Strict analysis retains only lowercase a–z candidates bounded by certain dots. Every rejected candidate breaks its adjacency run. The sensitivity projection selects the first alternative, flattens braces, lowercases letters and splits uncertain commas. Labels and non-P0 text are browsable but excluded from the fixed tests.
