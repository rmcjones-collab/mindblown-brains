RELEASE CANDIDATE 0.2.0-RC.1

Public evidence. Explicit boundaries.

Codex is an evidence-first research workspace, not an autonomous decipherment system. This candidate is prepared for owner review; this page does not assert that a public release has been authorized.

What is included

  • 19 network views: the Codex overview and 18 subject brains, with source-linked historical dossiers.
  • Voynich corpus ingestion: ZL3b and IT2a source bytes, raw loci, page metadata, loss-aware projections and version hashes.
  • 213 Yale canvas records, with preserved labels and on-demand image viewing. Combined pages and foldouts are not silently treated as single folios.
  • Two fixed structural tests, 16 comparisons, folio-group partitions, seeded null models, multiple-comparison correction and downloadable reproduction files.

The source record and digitized manuscript are maintained by Yale Digital Collections; transliteration conventions and editions are documented by Voynich.nu.

Rights and attribution

Transliteration files are included under the publisher’s explicit CC0 statement. The archived bytes retain their original headers; no EVA fonts or third-party annotation datasets are included.

Manuscript attribution: Cipher manuscript (Voynich manuscript). General Collection, Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library, Yale University. Image URLs are indexed, not relicensed; full scans load from Yale on demand, subject to Yale’s reuse and permissions policies.

Historical dossiers are short source-linked summaries, not copies of complete source publications. The application is not affiliated with or endorsed by the cited institutions. No open-source licence for the MindBlown application code is declared by this release candidate.

Privacy and external requests

The app includes public research content only. No personal MindBlown brains, communications, private destination catalog, credentials or analytics are bundled.

Text analysis and test execution occur in the page. Typed laboratory content is not uploaded by application code; exporting can include your text, vocabulary and notes, so review exports before sharing. Research notes and modifier scenarios are session-only. The shared renderer may retain local display preferences or saved layouts where browser storage is permitted; no remote account or synchronization exists.

The app requests D3 from jsDelivr, fonts from Fontshare and manuscript images from Yale. Those providers receive ordinary network requests. The research corpus and reference results are bundled; the app is not a fully offline website.

Research safeguards

Uncertainty, alternatives and exclusions remain visible. A rejected shuffle null is not a decipherment, proof of language or proof of encryption. Two transcriptions are not two independent manuscripts. The heldout partition is now revealed and must not be reused as untouched validation for new tuned claims.

Hash checks establish consistency with the bundled records, not authenticity against a malicious replacement. The fixed protocol is a local pre-execution freeze, not third-party preregistration. Independent scholarly review remains necessary.

Reproduce and review

Download the reproduction bundle, then run node --test test-analysis.mjs, node ingest.mjs and node reproduce.mjs --verify with Node 20 or later. The command-line test workflow needs no network, account or packages.
