The hardest open questions, joined at the seams.

This page is the index. The Network tab shows every brain on one screen with the shared neurons in the middle; open a brain for its full detail.

Each section of the question list (Physics & Cosmology, Life & Mind, Mathematics, Philosophy) becomes one or more brains, added one section at a time. What joins them is an observation made when the list was first drawn up: nearly every great open question sits at a seam between two frameworks that each work beautifully alone. Quantum theory and gravity. Chemistry and biology. Neurons and experience. Easy-to-check and easy-to-solve. The hardest questions are not gaps in data so much as gaps in the kind of explanation we know how to give. The shared neurons below are those seams, the kinds of gap, the frameworks on either side and the questions that belong to more than one section; 0 of them are already used by more than one brain.

Waiting for a second brain

Shared-vocabulary neurons that only one brain uses so far; the next brain that cites them lights up a connection across the network.

Built from one generator per brain (build_<brain>_data.py), build_network.py and build_pages.py; mb_shared.py holds the registry of shared ids and the list of sections and brains. Every synapse cites a public source, and the renderer (app.js, style.css) is the canonical MindBlown renderer, byte for byte.
